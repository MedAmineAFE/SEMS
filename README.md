# SEMS – Smart Energy Management System (V3.0)

## Overview

**SEMS V3.0** is an intelligent monitoring and diagnostic platform designed to optimize industrial and commercial energy consumption. The system processes real-time electrical data to identify energy waste, calculate carbon footprints, and generate automated audit reports to support facility managers in data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features

* **Real-Time Dashboard**
  Visualization of Active Power (*P*) and Reactive Power (*Q*) through interactive dashboards.

* **Automated Audit Generation**
  Instant report summaries including KPIs such as energy savings percentage, avoided CO₂ emissions, and financial gains.

* **Advanced Analytics**

  * Power Factor calculation (*cos φ*)
  * 24-hour load profile comparison (*Before vs. After optimization*)

* **Critical Alerting System**
  Real-time alerts when predefined power thresholds are exceeded to control peak demand.

* **Anomaly Detection**
  Automatic identification of abnormal consumption, including night-time energy waste during non-occupancy hours.

## Data Insights and Metrics

The platform imports time-series electrical data (CSV / Excel formats) and generates audit results such as:

* **Energy Savings**: 32.6% reduction in total energy consumption (tested scenarios)
* **Power Factor Optimization**: Improvement from **0.813** to **0.967**, reducing reactive power penalties
* **Environmental Impact**: Avoided CO₂ emissions up to **2215.50 kg**
* **Financial Analysis**: Direct savings reaching **8677.50 DH**

## Tech Stack

* **Backend**: Python (Flask / Django)
* **Data Processing**: Pandas, NumPy
* **Visualization**: Chart.js or Plotly (interactive 24h load profiles)
* **Documentation**: LaTeX (high-quality technical reports)
* **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Tailwind CSS)
